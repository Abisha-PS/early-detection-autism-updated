Stage 19: Privacy, Consent, & Ethical Framework

Before starting the interactive behavioural screening, please review how child data and sensory observations are protected.

⚠️

Important Clinical Disclaimer

This application is an AI-assisted behavioural screening tool designed for early identification and developmental triage. It is NOT a clinical diagnostic instrument and cannot independently diagnose Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). A definitive diagnosis requires comprehensive multidisciplinary clinical assessment, including standardized tools such as ADOS-2 administered by certified professionals.

Our Privacy Commitments:

  • No Raw Video/Audio Recording Storage: The webcam and microphone are used solely in real-time within your browser to extract mathematical and kinematic features (such as response latency, gaze angle proxy, and vocal pitch variability). Raw video streams are NEVER recorded, stored on disk, or uploaded to servers.
  • Anonymized Child ID: Every child profile is referenced strictly by an anonymous identifier (e.g. CH-2026-8912). No unnecessary personal identifiers are stored.
  • Role-Based Access: Only the authenticated parent and authorized consulting clinician may view the child's numerical behavioural profile.
  • Right to Deletion: Parents maintain the full right to delete their child's screening records and progress history at any time.
