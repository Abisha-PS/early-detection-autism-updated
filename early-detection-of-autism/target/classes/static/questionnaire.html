Stage 1: Child Profile & Developmental Observations

Complete this quick 10-item checklist to establish the child's developmental profile. Afterward, your child will be guided into 8 fun interactive play activities with Pip the Penguin!

ℹ️

Multimodal Screening Notice

Parent questionnaires provide essential context, but the questionnaire alone does not diagnose autism. Results are fused with non-invasive camera vision and voice response metrics from the child games.

👤 Child Demographic & Developmental Profile

📝 10 Behavioral Observations (Select the best match)

1. Social Smile: Does your child smile back at you when you smile or talk to them?

2. Eye Contact: Does your child make and sustain eye contact during conversation or play?

3. Response to Name: When you call your child's name from across the room, do they turn to look at you?

4. Pointing Interest: Does your child point with one finger to show you an interesting object or toy?

5. Shared Enjoyment: Does your child bring objects or toys to show you just to share excitement?

6. Imitation: Does your child copy simple everyday gestures (e.g. waving goodbye, clapping hands)?

7. Repetitive Movements: Does your child engage in repetitive hand flapping, spinning, or rocking?

8. Sensory Interests: Does your child show unusually intense fascination with spinning wheels, lights, or textures?

9. Play Flexibility: Does your child engage in imaginative pretend play rather than rigid repetitive lining up of toys?

10. Language Milestones: Does your child communicate using age-appropriate words or vocalizations?
